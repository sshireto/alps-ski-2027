Where we ski in January

A shortlist of Austrian resorts, scored on what we said matters: high and snow-sure, real après-ski, ski-in/ski-out, and a hotel with a spa for about €150 a night each.

Both are holds, not a final decision — free to cancel, so we can drop whichever one the group doesn't go with once we pick a week.

Best fit for us, in order

No resort ticks every box — this is our closest three.

1

Ischgl

Highest and liveliest of the three — 2,000–2,872 m keeps an El Niño winter from worrying us, and the Silvretta Arena après scene is the best of the shortlist. In-budget hotels mean a 5–10 min walk to the lift, not true ski-in/out.

2

Obertauern

Genuinely ski-in/out — every hotel sits on the piste — and the highest village in Austria. Trade-off is a smaller ski area and a spa hotel with real beds runs closer to €200 pp.

3

Zell am See – Kaprun

Cheapest of the three and the easiest to hit budget (650 m walk to the lift), with the Kitzsteinhorn glacier up to 3,030 m as snow insurance. Base village is lower at 750 m, so early-week conditions can be patchier.

When to go

Saturday is changeover day, so Sunday is the busiest ski day. School holidays roll in from 1 Feb and stack up all month.

10 – 30 Jan
Quietest weeks of the season, lowest prices, full snowpack. Our window.
1 – 6 Feb
Vienna & Lower Austria + Berlin holidays. Prices jump.
8 – 13 Feb
Bavaria, Carinthia, Saxony. Busy in Tyrol.
13 – 28 Feb
Netherlands, then Salzburg/Tyrol/Vorarlberg. Peak of the peak.

Good to know

El Niño winterNOAA gives >90 % odds of a very strong El Niño. For the Alps that usually means warmer with a higher snowline — the signal is noisy, but going high is the cheap insurance.
Booking directAustrian hotels' standard terms give free cancellation until 3 months before arrival. Ask for the terms in writing.
Ischgl terrainRed-heavy: 140 km red vs 47 km blue. Great for confident intermediates, thin for anyone who wants to cruise blues all day.
Getting thereMunich is the safe bet — many daily El Al flights, big rental fleet, 2–3 h to every resort on the list. Zurich beats Munich for Ischgl and St. Anton (2 h 15 vs 3 h). Vienna only makes sense for Schladming.
