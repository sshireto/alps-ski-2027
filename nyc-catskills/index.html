01 / The shape of the trip

A real change of scenery.
Not a checklist race.

The recommended version is a Hunter-area overnight. It keeps the lakes, the ski-lodge atmosphere and a different scenic road on the return, while protecting time to actually enjoy the hotel.

Locked-in plan.Road trip Friday, September 11 to Saturday, September 12, 2026. Two adults. Hertz pickup is 06:30 Friday at 126 West 55th Street, Manhattan. Overnight is confirmed at Matterhorn’s Hunter Lodge — Suite 13. All itinerary times are New York local time and driving durations remain planning allowances, not live traffic.

Main-route planning total: roughly 7–9 hours behind the wheel across both days, depending on the optional Perkins/Olana stops and Manhattan traffic. Saturday now uses the east-side Catskills/Hudson Valley loop rather than the old hiking route.

Rental details that matter

A Friday-morning / Saturday-evening hire exceeds 24 hours. Get a quote for the actual full duration, not an advertised one-day rate. Confirm office closing time, toll-program handling, coverage, fuel policy and driver-document requirements.

Pick up near the NYC hotel unless an alternative saves enough to justify the transfer. The plan does not assume an airport rental or include an airport-transfer detour.

Live checks before departure

Check park notices, the official lift operation page and navigation traffic. Entering Manhattan’s congestion zone may add a toll depending on the route; rental-company handling charges are separate.

07 / Keep the trip flexible

Two good reasons
to change the plan.

A lodge escape is only worth the drive if the room, weather and pace work. These are alternatives to the main trip, not extra items to squeeze into it.

Less driving / Bear Mountain

Keep the lakes.
Skip the Catskills.

Use the historic Bear Mountain Inn when you want an easier overnight. This keeps the lake section but removes the long northbound connection and the Woodstock return. Budget roughly 3–4 hours of total driving as an initial allowance, depending on your NYC start and traffic.

FridayLeave NYC after breakfast → Seven Lakes Drive → lake stops → lunch → historic-inn check-in around 15:00.
SaturdayBreakfast → a short Hessian Lake area walk or Perkins viewpoint if open → return to NYC without another long detour.

The official inn lists 15:00 check-in and 11:00 check-out. Current trail detours near the inn make posted signage important. This option does not include a ski-resort chairlift.

More hotel time / Windham

Make Wylder
the destination.

If the resort wins the hotel comparison, keep Friday’s lakes but simplify lunch and go straight to Windham. The pool, sauna and firepit are the reason for this version—not an extra drive back to Hunter for dinner.

FridayNYC → two lake stops → straightforward lunch → Wylder check-in / afternoon at the resort.
SaturdayChoose either a relaxed resort morning or an early transfer to Hunter, then descend toward Phoenicia and Woodstock.

Do not assume every facility is available: confirm operation, private-event restrictions and room access when booking. A Hunter outing adds a transfer of roughly 25–35 minutes from Windham.
