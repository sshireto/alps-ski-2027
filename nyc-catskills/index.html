01 / The shape of the trip

A real change of scenery.
Not a checklist race.

The recommended version is a Hunter-area overnight. It keeps the lakes, the ski-lodge atmosphere and a different scenic road on the return, while protecting time to actually enjoy the hotel.

Planning assumptions — confirm before booking.Arrive in NYC Wednesday, September 9; full days September 10–13. Road trip Friday, September 11 to Saturday, September 12, 2026. Two adults, one room, rental pickup near Midtown Manhattan. Your exact NYC hotel, budget and party size were not supplied. All itinerary times are New York local time.
The recommendation

Check Hunter Lodge first.

It fits the requested lodge feeling and keeps Saturday’s mountain outing close. Scribner’s remains on the shortlist, but a published September 11–12 private-event schedule needs checking before relying on it for a quiet stay.

The chairlift is the reason to keep Saturday in the mountains: the visitor board’s published fall schedule begins September 12. Operating conditions and ticket sales still need confirmation.

The rhythm

Drive, pause, then settle in.

Friday: leave early, enjoy two lake stops, take the Route 23A mountain approach, have lunch in Tannersville and stop driving for the evening.

Saturday: choose the chairlift or the falls, descend via Route 214, pause in Phoenicia and Woodstock, and leave for NYC around 14:30.

This is not the low-driving version. For that, use the Bear Mountain alternative below.

04 / Saturday, September 12

Mountains, then home.

Breakfast around 08:00–09:00. Pack the car and check out around 09:30. One main outing is enough before the scenic drive back.

10:00–11:15Saturday main outing
Recommended / ski-resort atmosphere

Hunter Mountain Scenic Skyride

After breakfast and a 09:30 check-out, drive about 5–10 minutes to the resort. Ride the open-air chairlift up and down and enjoy the mountain setting without committing to a substantial hike.

Published schedule: the county visitor board lists fall operation from September 12 to October 18, Saturdays and Sundays, 10:00–16:00, weather permitting. Reconfirm with Hunter Mountain before leaving the lodge.

Ticket price and availability: not verified. Use the official sales page. The fire-tower hike is not part of this itinerary.

Next: leave around 11:20; allow 35–45 minutes down Route 214 to Phoenicia.

09:55–10:40Alternative main outing
Choose instead of the chairlift / short walk

Kaaterskill Falls viewing platform

Leave Hunter around 09:30 and allow 15–25 minutes to the Laurel House Road parking area. Walk to the upper viewing platform and back: approximately 0.6 mile / 1 km round trip on a gravel route with some grade.

DEC describes the platform as 0.3 mile from the lot. This plan does not descend the steep trail to the base. Stay behind barriers and on designated paths; do not approach cliff edges or enter the water.

Cost: DEC lists no use fee. If the lot is full, do not park along Route 23A; parking restrictions apply. In heavy rain or unsafe conditions, skip the falls rather than using them as a bad-weather fallback.

06 / Keep the trip flexible

Two good reasons
to change the plan.

A lodge escape is only worth the drive if the room, weather and pace work. These are alternatives to the main trip, not extra items to squeeze into it.

Less driving / Bear Mountain

Keep the lakes.
Skip the Catskills.

Use the historic Bear Mountain Inn when you want an easier overnight. This keeps the lake section but removes the long northbound connection and the Woodstock return. Budget roughly 3–4 hours of total driving as an initial allowance, depending on your NYC start and traffic.

FridayLeave NYC after breakfast → Seven Lakes Drive → lake stops → lunch → historic-inn check-in around 15:00.
SaturdayBreakfast → a short Hessian Lake area walk or Perkins viewpoint if open → return to NYC without another long detour.

The official inn lists 15:00 check-in and 11:00 check-out. Current trail detours near the inn make posted signage important. This option does not include a ski-resort chairlift.
